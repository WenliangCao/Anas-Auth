## Description (≤ 4000)

Anas Auth is a native, open-source two-factor authenticator for iPhone and iPad. (Anas is Latin for "duck".)

It is built entirely with Apple's own frameworks, so it feels at home on iOS: it opens instantly, scrolls smoothly and follows the system look.

YOUR CODES STAY YOURS
• No account and no server of ours. Codes are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud database.
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
• Optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock. The app is blurred in the app switcher.
• Export an encrypted backup whenever you like.

EASY TO ADD, EASY TO MOVE
• Scan a QR code, pick a QR image from Photos, or enter the secret by hand.
• Import from Ente Auth, 2FAS, Aegis, andOTP, Bitwarden, Google Authenticator, Proton Authenticator, Raivo OTP, LastPass Authenticator and OTP Auth, or from a plain-text list of otpauth:// links.

MADE FOR EVERYDAY USE
• Tap a card to copy its code. Copied codes stay on this device and are cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds.
• See the next code before the current one runs out.
• Brand icons for thousands of services, matched automatically.
• Pin favorites, organize with tags, search, and sort by name, account, most used or recently used.
• Default and compact layouts, in light and dark mode.
• Supports TOTP, HOTP and Steam codes.
