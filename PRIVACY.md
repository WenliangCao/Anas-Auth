# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

*Last updated: 2026-09-28*

## English

Anas Auth does not collect any data.

- **Your codes.** Your two-factor accounts are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your device, they are also synced through your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database, which only you can access. We have no servers and cannot see your data.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and makes no network requests other than iCloud sync handled by iOS.
- **Camera and Photos.** The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Images you pick from Photos are only used to read a QR code. Both are processed on your device and are never uploaded.
- **Face ID / Touch ID.** Handled entirely by iOS. The app only receives a success or failure result.
- **Backups.** Backup files are created only when you export one, and are saved where you choose.

Questions: https://github.com/WenliangCao/Anas-Auth/issues

## 中文

Anas Auth 不收集任何数据。

- **你的验证码**：两步验证账号保存在你的设备上。如果设备开启了 iCloud，还会通过你私人的 iCloud（CloudKit）数据库同步，只有你自己能访问。我们没有任何服务器，也无法看到你的数据。
- **不统计、不追踪**：App 不含任何统计分析、广告或追踪代码。除了由 iOS 负责的 iCloud 同步，App 不发起任何网络请求。
- **相机与相册**：相机只用于扫描二维码；从相册选取的图片只用于识别二维码。两者都只在设备上处理，从不上传。
- **Face ID / 触控 ID**：完全由 iOS 处理，App 只会得到验证成功或失败的结果。
- **备份**：只有在你主动导出时才会生成备份文件，并保存到你选择的位置。

问题反馈：https://github.com/WenliangCao/Anas-Auth/issues
