# Anas Auth Support

Anas Auth is a native, open-source two-factor authenticator for iPhone and iPad.

[中文技术支持](#anas-auth-技术支持) · [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)

## Get help

If you find a problem or want to suggest an improvement, please [open a support issue](https://github.com/WenliangCao/Anas-Auth/issues) or email [contact@veiledcat.eu.org](mailto:contact@veiledcat.eu.org).

When reporting a problem, include your iOS or iPadOS version, device model, Anas Auth version, and a short description of what happened. Please hide real account details and authentication codes in screenshots, and do not send secrets, QR codes, passwords, or backup files.

## Privacy

Anas Auth does not collect your data. Your authenticator accounts are stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced through your own private iCloud database. The app does not require an Anas Auth account and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## Source code

Anas Auth is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license. [View the source code](https://github.com/WenliangCao/Anas-Auth).

# Anas Auth 技术支持

Anas Auth 是一款原生、开源的 iPhone 和 iPad 两步验证器。

## 获取帮助

如果你遇到问题或希望提出改进建议，请[提交支持问题](https://github.com/WenliangCao/Anas-Auth/issues)，或发送邮件至 [contact@veiledcat.eu.org](mailto:contact@veiledcat.eu.org)。

反馈问题时，请附上 iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、设备型号、Anas Auth 版本以及简短的问题说明。截图中请隐藏真实账号信息和验证码；请勿发送密钥、二维码、密码或备份文件。

## 隐私

Anas Auth 不收集你的数据。两步验证账号保存在你的设备上；开启 iCloud 时，会通过你自己的私有 iCloud 数据库同步。App 无需注册 Anas Auth 账号，没有广告、统计分析或追踪。

查看完整的[隐私政策](privacy.html#anas-auth-隐私政策)。

## 源代码

Anas Auth 以 GNU AGPL-3.0 许可开源。[查看完整源代码](https://github.com/WenliangCao/Anas-Auth)。
