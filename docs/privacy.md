# Anas Auth Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

[中文隐私政策](#anas-auth-隐私政策) · [Support](./)

This policy describes how Anas Auth handles your information, and how this support website and support requests are handled.

## Data collection

The Anas Auth app does not collect personal data or send your authenticator accounts, secrets, or codes to the developer. We have no servers for storing your authenticator data and cannot access that data.

## Your accounts and iCloud

Your authenticator accounts and secrets are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your device, they are also synced through your own private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account. Without iCloud, the app works locally.

iCloud sync is provided by Apple and is subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Anas Auth does not operate its own sync service or require an Anas Auth account.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Anas Auth contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Apart from iCloud sync handled by iOS, the app does not make network requests.

## Camera, Photos, and app lock

The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Images you select from Photos are used only to read QR codes. Both are processed on your device and are not uploaded by Anas Auth.

Face ID, Touch ID, and device passcode authentication are handled by iOS. Anas Auth receives only the authentication result, not your biometric data or device passcode.

## Backups, retention, and deletion

Backup files are created only when you export one and are saved where you choose. You may export an encrypted backup or a plain-text backup.

Your authenticator entries remain in your local app storage and, when enabled, your private iCloud database until you delete them. You can delete individual entries within Anas Auth. Deletions are synced to your other devices when iCloud sync is available.

Exported backups are separate copies. Deleting an entry in the app does not remove it from a previously exported backup; you can delete those files from the location where you saved them. The developer cannot delete your local files or private iCloud data on your behalf.

## This website and support requests

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to it. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as described in its [GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you contact us by email or open a GitHub issue, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to and resolve your request. GitHub issues are public and are also subject to GitHub's privacy statement. Please do not send authentication secrets, QR codes, passwords, or backup files.

If you want us to delete information you provided in a support request, contact us at the email address below. Copies managed by third-party services are subject to those services' policies.

## Changes and contact

If Anas Auth's privacy practices change, this page and its update date will be updated to reflect the changes.

For privacy questions, email [contact@veiledcat.eu.org](mailto:contact@veiledcat.eu.org). For general help, visit [Anas Auth Support](./).

# Anas Auth 隐私政策

**更新日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日**

本政策说明 Anas Auth 如何处理你的信息，以及本技术支持网站和支持请求的信息处理方式。

## 数据收集

Anas Auth App 不收集个人数据，也不会将你的两步验证账号、密钥或验证码发送给开发者。我们没有存储两步验证数据的服务器，也无法访问这些数据。

## 你的账号与 iCloud

两步验证账号和密钥保存在你的设备上。如果设备开启了 iCloud，还会通过与你的 Apple 账号关联的私有 CloudKit 数据库同步。不使用 iCloud 时，App 可以完全在本地使用。

iCloud 同步由 Apple 提供，适用 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)。Anas Auth 不运营自己的同步服务，也不要求注册 Anas Auth 账号。

## 统计分析、广告和追踪

Anas Auth 不含统计分析、广告或追踪代码。除了由 iOS 负责的 iCloud 同步，App 不发起网络请求。

## 相机、相册与 App 锁

相机仅用于扫描二维码；从相册选择的图片仅用于读取二维码。两者均在你的设备上处理，Anas Auth 不会上传这些内容。

Face ID、触控 ID 和设备密码验证由 iOS 处理。Anas Auth 只接收验证结果，不会获取你的生物识别数据或设备密码。

## 备份、保留与删除

只有在你主动导出时才会生成备份文件，文件保存在你选择的位置。你可以导出加密备份或明文备份。

两步验证条目会保留在 App 的本地存储中；启用 iCloud 时，也会保留在你的私有 iCloud 数据库中，直到你删除它们。你可以在 Anas Auth 中删除单个条目。iCloud 同步可用时，删除操作会同步到你的其他设备。

导出的备份是独立副本。在 App 中删除条目不会自动从之前导出的备份中移除该条目；你可以自行在备份保存位置删除这些文件。开发者无法代你删除本地文件或私有 iCloud 数据。

## 本网站与支持请求

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，我们没有添加统计分析、广告或追踪脚本。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址，具体见 [GitHub Pages 说明](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)及其[隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。

如果你通过邮件联系或提交 GitHub Issue，我们会接收你主动提供的信息，并用于回复和解决你的请求。GitHub Issue 是公开的，同时适用 GitHub 的隐私声明。请勿发送身份验证密钥、二维码、密码或备份文件。

如果希望我们删除你在支持请求中提供的信息，请通过下方邮箱联系。由第三方服务管理的副本适用相应服务的政策。

## 政策变更与联系方式

如果 Anas Auth 的隐私处理方式发生变化，本页面及更新日期会随之更新。

如有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 [contact@veiledcat.eu.org](mailto:contact@veiledcat.eu.org)。一般使用问题请查看[技术支持页面](./)。
